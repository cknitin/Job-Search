# Job agent roadmap

The goal is more interviews, not more applications. Track **interviews per 10 applications**.

## Fix first

- [ ] Verify resume facts: "17+ years" against dates showing about 19, the 2011–12 cloud claims, team sizes "15+" and "20+"
- [ ] Test the Chrome extension and auto apply on real sites
- [ ] Commit the work, after checking what would become public

## Next features

| Feature | What it does | Effort | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Follow-up reminders | Flags applications with no reply after 7 days and drafts a message | Small | Built |
| Skill gap report | Counts what target jobs ask for that your resume does not show | Small | Built |
| Interview prep | Likely questions and answers from the job, your resume and your notes | Medium | Built |
| Outcome learning | Shows which sites, job titles and fit scores lead to replies and interviews | Medium | Built |
| Referral helper | Drafts a short note to someone you know at the company; you send it | Small | Built |
| Job alert import | Ranks the jobs in an alert email you download and select | Medium | Built |
| Smart paste | Paste a whole page; the AI picks out the fields | Small | Built |

## If other people will use it

- Resume upload instead of editing YAML files
- Any profession, not only architect roles
- Separate accounts, secure key storage and encrypted data
- Installer or hosted version, with automated tests
- Legal review of the extension and each job source

## Avoid

- Mass auto apply, and bots on LinkedIn, Naukri or Indeed: account risk and weak applications
- Letting the AI add skills you do not have
